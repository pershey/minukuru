App Review Note (English):
This in-app purchase is a non-consumable product named "Minukuru Premium".
It unlocks premium features in the app:
- additional question content beyond the free set
- removal of the ad area in the free version
- skip answered questions mode
- reality mode for advanced training content

How to access it:
1. Launch the app "Minukuru".
2. Tap the "Premium" button on the title screen.
3. The dedicated premium page opens immediately.
4. The purchase option for "Minukuru Premium" is shown there.

No login or account creation is required.
The attached screenshot shows the title screen entry point for the Premium purchase flow on iPad.
